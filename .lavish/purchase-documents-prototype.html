What do the two purchasing documents look like on the page?

Round 4 - final check
What you decided, drawn as the final documents
  • Daftar Pesanan: one document printed twice. Continuous ledger, 32 mm photo, one variant per line, quantity, Diterima box, empty Catatan column, total units and two signature lines. No price, no SKU code.
  • Reprint (tab 2): only the current quantities, no trace of the old ones; lines cut to 0 disappear. I kept a "Dicetak" date in the header so two prints can be told apart.
  • 1688 purchase (tab 3): the same document; the header adds the Chinese local courier and tracking number when they are known. No forwarder, no marking code.
  • Forwarder invoice (tab 4): classic table, 发票 / INVOICE, marking code on top, forwarder warehouse and address, supplier phone, courier and tracking number.
Proposal for a shortfall the supplier will resend
  1. The short line closes with a new outcome, To be resent, beside Refunded and Loss. It no longer counts as on order.
  2. niskora keeps the owed units per supplier and shows them on the supplier and in restock advice for that supplier.
  3. When you start the next purchase order for that supplier, niskora offers the owed units as resend lines: no price, marked "kirim ulang" with the old order number (see Tempat Sampah, Biru in tab 1). The supplier is reminded on paper, the unloading team counts them, and they count as on order so restock advice does not order them twice.
  4. When they arrive, the old shortfall is settled. If they are short again, the resend line gets its own outcome. You can also turn an owed line into Refunded or Loss at any time.
